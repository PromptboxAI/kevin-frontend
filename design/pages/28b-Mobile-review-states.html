What each tap opens

Four states of the same screen, side by side. Every control shown here is live — these are the real menus, rendered with one already open so they can be reviewed together. Tapping around inside any phone works.

Tap a photo row
Photo detail
The primary path. Large image, filename and time, the room as a tappable field, and a 120-character additional-identification note. Prev/next walk the whole session without closing; Exclude from processing and Delete act on this frame; a note already written shows as a chip on the row behind.
Per-row ⋯
Photo actions
A shortcut past the detail view when you already know what you want: Assign room · Exclude from processing · Delete. Excluded rows dim, gain a "Won't process" pill, and drop out of the process count — the photo stays on the claim as a context shot.
Header ⋯
Session sheet
Bulk cleanup for the whole shoot: Remove duplicates · Remove blurry photos · Assign rooms to untagged, which queues every untagged frame and walks them in turn · Discard session, gated behind a confirm.
Assign room / Rename
Room sheet
Free-text room plus recent-room chips. Opened from a group header it retags every photo in that group at once — grouping is the multi-select.
